transport, correlation, lifetime and observation. H retired that duplicate runtime,
installer and desktop launch policy after Rust replacement admission. Its source,
including `desktop-bridge-615.patch`, remains in experiment history at `ab41733`
and the retained experiment branches; no historical product snapshot is imported.

The production-candidate Rust parser, Responses service, lane journal and retained

their source qualification and licenses remain in preserved installation/proof.

not vendored here. Source integration does not grant redistribution rights for
third-party desktop application binaries.
